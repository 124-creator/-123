Dear Editors,

Please consider “Bid and award dispersion in European carbon auctions” by Zhongfei Tian and Xiang Wang for Empirical Economics.

The paper examines conditional co-variation between quantity-dispersion measures reported for 1,281 successful EUA auctions during 2020–2025. It separates marginal indicator levels from their conditional association, and documents how a pooled relationship differs across two historical periods. A supplementary overlap-weighted comparison retains a positive period contrast while exposing incomplete balance of auction-volume distributions. An additional 2026 sample is reported as limited new-time evidence, not a forecasting exercise.

The intended contribution is a measurement-explicit empirical description of public auction quantities. We do not claim a causal allocation effect, a new estimator, or price informativeness. Exploratory choices, the short time axis of the extension, and the limits of exact-snapshot data access are disclosed. Code, source identifiers and diagnostic records accompany the study.
