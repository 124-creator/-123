# Manuscript revision scope

This directory contains an author-review manuscript, not a submitted paper. Empirical Economics is a working fit reference, not certified CAS-zone eligibility.

Preserve the frozen input and result commits. No new regression, resampling, sample restriction or market data was introduced during v2 writing. Do not turn association into causality, authenticated population CV, concentration compression, procurement value or forecasting gains.

Do not remove the historical-exploration disclosure, A=B explanation, theta/partial-R2 dependence, interval levels or period heterogeneity. Do not invent author details or absence of conflicts/funding. New journal text reads must retain access/version levels; do not retry denied routes by circumvention.

Editing/build scripts only format existing sources. They are not experiment runners. _build and QA renders are not publication deliverables. Raw third-party data and full source articles are not to be added.

Before pushing, compare with the actual branch head and add only this paper directory. Never force-update or alter old results/main. Run local numeric and document QA; do not claim full repository validation merely from manuscript checks.
