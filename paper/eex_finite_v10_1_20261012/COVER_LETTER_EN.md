# Cover letter — prepared, not sent

Dear Editors,

We submit for consideration our manuscript, “Quantity dispersion and reported price deviations in European carbon auctions”. The study examines what reported quantity dispersion adds to the description of completed European carbon auctions, separating the co-variation of bid and award statistics from their association with an official price-comparison outcome.

The analysis uses 1,281 successful EUA auctions during 2020–2025 and a matched subset of 240 German auctions with official reported-price comparisons. The bid–award residual correlation is close to 0.65. In the German subset, a difference equal to the observed interquartile range of transformed bid dispersion corresponds to a fitted signed-price-deviation difference of −14.51 basis points, conditional on conventional auction indicators and the same-auction price level. The manuscript also reports that the absolute-deviation result is imprecise and that the signed estimate is sensitive in magnitude to 2022. These restrictions are integral to the contribution rather than omitted adverse diagnostics.

We believe the distinction between quantity levels, conditional co-variation and signed versus absolute price deviations is relevant to the journal's empirical relationship and measurement readership. The paper does not claim causal efficiency gains or forecast performance. Its retrospective research sequence, data-source qualifications and generative AI assistance are disclosed. Editable documents, figure sources, code locators and aggregate numerical records accompany the manuscript. Access to the author-held price inputs remains subject to provider permission, as set out in the data statement.

The research received no financial support, and the authors declare no financial or non-financial interests relevant to the article. Zhongfei Tian prepared and finally revised the manuscript; Xiang Wang critically reviewed and checked it and was responsible for final-version approval. We prefer the traditional subscription publication route.
