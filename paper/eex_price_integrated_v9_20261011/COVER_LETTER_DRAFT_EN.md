Dear Editors of Empirical Economics,

We would like to submit “Quantity dispersion and reported price deviations in European carbon auctions” for consideration as a research article. The manuscript examines what public auction quantity reports reveal beyond marginal totals and participation counts. It combines the conditional relationship between reported bid and award dispersion in 1,281 successful EUA auctions with a separate, matched German price analysis of 240 auctions.

The quantity relationship is positive but varies across historical periods. The German analysis finds a negative conditional association between bid dispersion and the official-reported signed percentage deviation beyond the specified conventional auction controls. The manuscript reports the non-definitive absolute-deviation result and the substantial sensitivity of coefficient magnitude to 2022 alongside the primary result. It does not claim causal efficiency, forecast gains, matched-bidder distributional change or a new estimator. These distinctions are central to the contribution, rather than caveats added after the conclusions.

The paper provides fixed protocols, source locators, code and aggregate results. Original row-level price material remains subject to a lawful access arrangement that must be finalized before submission. The investigation is retrospective and exploratory, and AI assistance is disclosed in the Methods section. The authors received no financial support.
