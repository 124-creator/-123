# Cover letter — draft, not submitted

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, “Bid and award dispersion in European carbon auctions: Conditional association and temporal heterogeneity, 2020–2025”, for publication as a research article in Empirical Economics.

The paper examines how reported bid and award dispersion co-vary in 1,281 successful EUA primary auctions during 2020–2025. The contribution is a conditional and period-specific description of public quantity statistics. Residual correlations are approximately 0.65 under two transformations. Their strength is greater in the later historical period, even though both reported ratios have lower marginal medians. The distinction between indicator levels and conditional co-variation is central to the interpretation.

The study complements price-focused auction research rather than introducing a new estimator. It makes the ratio components explicit, reports measurement and influence diagnostics, and distinguishes the protocol's primary slope contrasts from secondary correlation contrasts. The retrospective, exploratory design and unresolved historical moment-population metadata are stated in the manuscript. We do not interpret the estimates as causal allocation effects or matched-bidder inequality changes.

The manuscript and Online Resource 1 provide numerical provenance and reproduction paths. The final data-access statement will respect EEX's applicable rights and terms.
