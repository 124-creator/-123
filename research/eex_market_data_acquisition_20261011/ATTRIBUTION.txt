The original EU carbon price data.xlsx is reproduced byte-for-byte, unchanged, under that deposit-level declared license. The parsed CSV is a derived representation: Excel dates become ISO dates and sheet/row locators are added. No price adjustment, imputation, weekend removal, interpolation, model prediction or rolling-contract reconstruction is performed. The same attribution and CC BY 4.0 apply to this derived price table. No author endorsement is implied.

Neither the deposit title nor byte identity authenticates a specific exchange, allowance, contract, price definition or currency. The original workbook only labels its two fields date and Y. These unresolved semantics are disclosed in SOURCE_CONTRACT.json and results/DAILY_AUDIT.json.
